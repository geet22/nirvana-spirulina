Our Story

Where Ancient Land
Meets Living Science

In 2011, our founders stood on the edge of the Deccan Plateau and asked a question that would change everything: what if India's most generous land could produce the world's most powerful nutrition?

Aurangabad — home to the Ajanta & Ellora Caves and centuries of civilisation — holds a secret that is invisible to the naked eye. Its alkaline groundwater, drawn from the Godavari basin, registers a natural pH of 9.5 — the precise environment spirulina has evolved over 3.5 billion years to thrive in. No chemical intervention required.

Add 300 days of Maharashtra sunshine and diurnal temperatures that mirror optimal cultivation conditions, and you have something extraordinary: spirulina with a protein content 15% above international benchmarks.
